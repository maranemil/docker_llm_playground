how to create a google sheet for newsletter content for free

To create a free newsletter content tracker or calendar using Google Sheets, open Google Sheets, start a blank spreadsheet, and set up clear column headers for your content plan like Date, Topic, Draft Status, and Copy.Setting Up Your Newsletter SheetGo to Google Sheets and click Blank to open a new sheet.Create column headers in Row 1: Date, Edition Name, Topic / Headline, Draft Status, Writer, and Notes / Links.Format the header row with bold text and a background color so it stands out.Turn on text wrapping for content columns so long copy or notes fit neatly inside the cells.Organizing Your Content WorkflowAdd dropdown menus for status tracking (e.g., Idea, Writing, Review, Scheduled, Published). Select your status cells, go to Data > Data validation, and add items.Use separate tabs at the bottom of the sheet for different months, or keep a master list sorted by upcoming publication dates.Share the sheet with team members or co-writers by clicking the Share button in the top right corner.
